# Chapter Two: The Backward Tide

**A time-travel puzzle adventure on one shore, in three ages.** The second chapter of [Chronoheart](../README.md). It opens once [Chapter One](chapter-1-hollowmere.md) is finished.

Three weeks after Vivienne restarted the Clocktower of Hollowmere, the river barge ties up at **Saltwick**, where the valley meets the sea. Or where it used to. The tide went out the night the Tower restarted, and it has not come back. Donald Eugene's old pocketwatch has started ticking **backward**, and it pulls toward the headland: toward the **Tide Well**, where a clock far older than the Tower keeps the sea's own time.

Vivienne is the keeper of the **Chronoheart** now, and it is mended: she can stay in any age for as long as she likes.

| Age | What it looks like | Its color |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Past** (the Age of Tides) | the tide folk's stilt village, coral, a full turquoise sea right up against the road | sea-green |
| **Present** (Saltwick) | a harbor town with no harbor: mud from wall to wall, boats aground | pale blue |
| **Future** (the Dry Age) | salt flats where the sea was, rusted hulks, a salvage robot with nothing to salvage | rust orange |

The Future you walk through is the one that happens **if you fail**. Mend the Tide Clock, and you get to watch it become something else.

## How to play

- **Travel through time.** `Q` steps one age back, `E` one age forward (on touch: swipe). The Chronoheart no longer runs down: explore as long as you like.
- **You can't switch into something solid**, or over a drop with nothing to stand on: the amulet shakes and refuses, and shows you what's in the way.
- **The time lens:** hold `Tab` (or hold the amulet dial on touch) to open a round window onto a neighboring age, live, and walk about with it.
- **Clean sea water is safe to swim in** (hold Jump to rise, let go to sink). **Mud and the tide-rip are not**: they cost a heart and put you back on solid ground.
- **Hearts:** you start with 5. Each finished favor adds one.
- **Clock shrines** are checkpoints. Walking past one saves and heals you. The game also saves after every important step.
- **The Menu** (`I`, or the ☰ button) shows your current goal, a **Hint** if you're stuck (`G` opens it directly), your items and your favors.
- **Lost Cogs** are optional collectibles: 12 in all, and this time they are worth something.

### What the Chronoheart learns in Saltwick

| Power | From | The rule, in one sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rewind** | Michelle the Wise, at the head of the Long Pier | Hold `R` and the pocketwatch runs **you** backward along the path you just took (up drops, across gaps, back through era shifts), while the rest of the world keeps going. |
| **Reach** | The Keeper's Loupe, from Odalys the Lightkeeper | While the time lens is open, **Interact** and **Attack** go *through* it: use, take or strike something in the other age from where you stand in this one. |

The amulet's ring shows how much path the watch remembers (about 8 seconds of your own movement; standing still costs nothing), and a faint gold trail shows it on the ground.

## Controls

### Keyboard

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `A` `D` | Move left / right |
| `W` `S` | Move into / out of the scene; climb ladders |
| `Space` | Jump (hold for a higher jump); hold to swim upward |
| `Shift` | Sprint |
| `C` | Dodge-roll |
| `X` | Swing your wrench: at crabs, rats and drones, and at bells |
| `F` | Interact: talk to people, use things |
| `Q` / `E` | Travel one era back / forward |
| `Tab` | Time lens: hold to open a window onto another age (`Q`/`E` choose which) |
| hold `R` | Rewind *(once learned)* |
| `Tab` + `F` / `X` | Reach through the lens *(once you have the Loupe)* |
| `I` | Menu: goal, hint, items, favors |
| `G` | Open the Menu with the hint showing |
| `H` | Controls |
| `Esc` / `P` | Pause |

### Touch (phones and tablets)

On-screen controls appear automatically on touch screens.

- **Joystick:** touch anywhere on the left half and drag. Push all the way to sprint.
- **Buttons:** Jump, Attack, Roll, and **Interact** (only when there's something to use). **Rewind** (hold) appears once learned.
- **Swipe** anywhere else: left to travel toward the Future, right toward the Past. You can also tap the era names at the top.
- **Hold the amulet** to open the time lens; with the Loupe, Interact and Attack then go through it.
- It plays best in landscape.

## Walkthrough

> **Spoilers ahead.** The in-game **Hint** gives the same answers one step at a time.

The road runs west to east along the shore. The harbor rhyme sets the goal: *"Dawn and Noon and Dusk must ring, before the Well lets anything in."* Each age hung one bell for the sea.

### 1. Saltwick Quay

- Talk to **Donald Eugene** by the barge and **Harbormaster Brannoch** a little way east.
- All three favors start here (see [Favors](#favors)).

### 2. The Mudflats

- In the Present, the harbor is sinking mud. Two ways across:
  - **Past:** it is clean, deep sea. Walk in and swim.
  - **Future:** it is dry. Ladders lead down to the harbor floor (two drones live there) and up the far side.
- In the **Present**, hop from the west bank onto the deck of the **Marigold**, the boat aground just offshore, and take the **old fishing nets**. You'll want them later.

### 3. The Long Pier: the Noon Bell

- **Michelle the Wise** is waiting at the head of the pier. She teaches the pocketwatch to **Rewind**.
- A raised quay deck runs out to **Bell Rock**, through the **tide gate**. The gate opens for 3.5 seconds when its lever is pulled, but the lever is down on the shore, and the only way back up is the ladder at the far west end.
- So: climb the ladder and walk along the deck **to the gate first**. Step off the front of the deck beside it, pull the lever below, and **hold `R`**. The watch runs you straight back up to the gate. Walk through. (It locks open behind you.)
- On top of Bell Rock, hit the **Noon Bell** three times with your wrench.

### 4. The Saltings

- **Salvage Pit 3** (Future): a pit with no ladder and no exit, and the **Dusk Bell's clapper** lying in it. Jump in, take it, and rewind out. The watch never forgets the way into a pit, however long you spend in it. (You leave a rope ladder behind afterward.)
- **The sea wall** is too sheer to climb in any age, though it's lowest in the Future. In the **Present**, string the nets across the **net-drying posts** beside it. A time-lapse shows three hundred years of wind heaping a dune against them. In the **Future**, walk up the dune and over the wall.

### 5. Keeper's Light: the Dawn Bell

- The lighthouse lamp went out the night the tide did: its heart is a **moon pearl**, and it has clouded over. **Odalys the Lightkeeper** gives you a **chip** of it.
- In the **Past**, the pool beside the sea stack is full of water, and the **Mother Clam** lies on its floor. Dive down and give her the chip. A time-lapse: six hundred years, one layer at a time.
- In the **Present**, climb down the ladder into the dried-out pool and take the **moon pearl** from the fossil clam. Bring it to Odalys.
- She relights the lamp, unlocks the **Keeper's stair**, and gives you her great-great-grandmother's **Loupe**: **Reach**.
- Climb the ladder up the front of the sea stack, then the one up the tower, to the **gallery**. In the Past, the Dawn Bell hangs from a mast exactly here, out over the water, with nothing beneath it to stand on. Open the lens on the **Past** (`Tab`, then `Q`) and swing your wrench through it, three times.

### 6. The Dry Lock

A channel no age can ford: a tide-rip in the Past, mud in the Present, a dry canyon in the Future. Only the Future's footbridge crosses it, behind a door that opens for 3.5 seconds. The door's control panel hangs out over the drop, where nobody can stand. But in the Present, an old jetty still reaches that far.

1. In the **Future**, climb the ladder to the walkway and stand **at the door**.
2. Step off the walkway's front edge. Travel to the **Present**, and walk out to the end of the jetty.
3. Open the lens on the **Future** (`Tab`, then `E`): the panel hangs right in front of you. Press `F` to reach through and press it.
4. **Hold `R`.** The watch runs you back along the jetty, back into the Future, and up to the door while it's still open.

### 7. The Warden's Wharf: the Dusk Bell

- The Dusk Bell stands in the middle of the **Future** wharf, with a **Warden** walking a loop around it. (Saltwick has one too.) Stay out of its sight cone and the searchlight; bollards block its view.
- In the Present, that spot is inside the boathouse, so walk most of the way in the **Present**, along the back or the front of the road, then step into the Future beside the bell.
- Fit the **clapper**, then ring it: three strokes. **Every stroke brings the Warden running.** Ring, then rewind back the way you came, right out of its age. It soon gives up. (Or be quick, and land all three before it reaches you.)

### 8. Gull's Head

- Climb the long stair. In the **Past**, **Maren the Tide-Reader** is building a clock for the sea, and has no heart for it yet.
- With all three notes, the Well's door opens, in the **Present** only.

### 9. The Tide Well

Four terraces step down to the heart of the Tide Clock, with a gate on the edge of each. Only the Present's gates still move. A shrine saves at the door, and each gate leaves a ladder behind it.

1. **The first gate (ages, one step at a time).** Its lever is on a builders' scaffold high on the back wall, in the Past. In the **Future**, fallen masonry by the door makes a stair up to that height. At the top, step back to the **Present** (two planks of the old scaffold still hold), then on into the **Past**, and throw the lever. It stays thrown for six hundred years.
2. **The second gate (Reach, twice).** Its winch is walled into an alcove in the Past; the winch's crank is locked in a cage in the Future. In the Present, stand where the cage is and take the crank **through the lens**. Then stand where the alcove is, and fit it and wind it through the lens.
3. **The last gate (Rewind, across an age).** Its lever holds it open for 3.5 seconds, and the lever is in a pit in the **Future**, with no way out. Stand by the gate in the Present first; walk west to the middle of the terrace, step into the Future, and drop into the pit. Pull the lever and hold `R`: up out of the pit, back into the Present, back to the gate.
4. **The heart.** At the foot of the great wheel is an empty cradle, the size of a pocketwatch.

Then the tide comes in. **Hold Jump and swim up**, west over the terraces, and out the way you came.

## Favors

Three optional side quests. Each gives you **+1 heart**. Nobody you meet knows you're traveling through time, but what you do for them is remembered.

| Favor | Starts with | What to do | What it leaves behind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A light for the tide folk** | Isha the Shell-Singer (Past, the Quay) | Her people's lamps blow out in the first wind. **DIGBY** (Future, the Quay) has four thousand lumen cells and no ships: ask him. Bring one to Isha. | The **Ever-Lantern** hangs on the quay in every age after. |
| **Pim's whistle** | Pim (Past, east end of the Quay) | She dropped her best shell whistle in the middle of the harbor. In the **Past**, dive to the harbor floor in the Mudflats and bring it back. | Talk to Brannoch afterward: he has started wondering whose whistle he's been blowing for thirty years. |
| **Zach's lost hoard** | Zach Tiberious (Present, the Quay) | He buried four hundred acorns somewhere along the shore, and forgot where. Look at the back of the road at the west end of the Saltings, in the **Future**. Then tell him. | The only green thing in the Dry Age. |

## After the story

- **Your save is kept.** Choose Continue and walk the shore with the tide back in. The Present's mud is sea again (swim it), the boats are afloat, and the Future is not the Dry Age any more.
- **The Lost Cogs matter.** Finish with all twelve and Donald Eugene has exactly enough for a watch movement: you keep **Rewind** for good. Find the rest afterward and bring them to him on the quay, and he'll build it then.
- Talk to everyone again. Maren, in the Past, has something to show you.

## What changed since Chapter One

The design critique of the first chapter, and what this one does about each point:

| Chapter One | Chapter Two |
| --- | --- |
| The 10-second Chronoheart drain fought the pleasure of looking around another age. | The Chronoheart is mended. No drain, ever. |
| The Echo Gallery was hard to *read*: five rules at once. | Two new powers, each one sentence long, each taught alone, then used twice more before the finale combines them. |
| A heavy control surface (about 14 actions, a tap/hold key for Tempo). | One new button (hold to Rewind). Reach reuses the lens, Interact and Attack. Seal, Echo and Tempo are gone. |
| The wrench was only for enemies. | The three bells are rung with it, one of them through the lens. |
| Areas were sealed locks with no cross-area consequences. | The nets from the Mudflats build the dune in the Saltings; the clapper from the Saltings rings the bell on the Wharf; the Future itself is the consequence. |
| The finale switched off era travel. | The Tide Well uses all three ages and both powers. |
| Finishing wiped the save; ten cogs earned one line of text. | The save is kept, the world changes, and twelve cogs earn Rewind for good. |

Carried over on purpose: time-lapses that show cause becoming effect, kindnesses that are remembered in later ages, "you can always go back", hints one step at a time, American spelling.
