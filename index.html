Controls

JoystickTouch anywhere on the left side and drag. Push all the way to sprint. Up and down move into and out of the scene and climb ladders. JumpHold for a higher jump · hold to swim upward AttackSwing your wrench: at crabs, rats and drones, and at bells RollDodge-roll InteractTalk to people and use things. It appears when there's something to interact with. You can also tap the prompt. SwipeSwipe left to travel toward the Future, right toward the Past. Drag slowly to preview first. Past · FutureOr tap the era names at the top AmuletHold the amulet dial to open the time lens, a window onto another era around you, then tap Past or Future to choose which SealLights up beside something that shimmers. Tap to hold it in its moment, unchanged in every later age. EchoIn the Present: tap to start recording, tap again to stop (up to 5 seconds). Your echo repeats those moves, over and over. Up to 3 at once. Stand on an echo and tap Dispel echo to remove it. LanternTap to light it or put it out: inside its light, another age is real (its floors, its bridges, its ice). While lit, swipe or tap Past / Future to swing the Lantern's light between the ages. EchoTap to start recording yourself, tap again to stop. Your echo repeats those moves, over and over (up to 3 at once, or 4 with Vivienne's Pocketwatch), and remembers the Lantern's light if it was lit! Stand on an echo and tap Dispel echo to remove it. Slow · FastTempo: tap to slow down or speed up the machines near you. Uses the Chronoheart's ring. RewindHold to run yourself backward along the path you just took: up drops, across gaps, back through era shifts. Only you: the world keeps going. The amulet's ring shows how much the watch remembers. ReachWhile the time lens is open, Interact and Attack go through it: use, take or strike something in the other age. DialogueTap anywhere to continue MenuYour current goal (tap Hint if you're stuck), your items and your favors. The game pauses while it's open.

Push blocks by walking into them. The Chronoheart drains while you're in the Past or Future and recharges in the Present.

The Chronoheart is mended: stay in any age for as long as you like. Clean sea water is safe to swim in. Mud, and the tide-rip, are not.

Where frost lies on the ground, the ages are frozen together: you can't shift there, but the Lantern works. Climb ladders with W (or up on the joystick).
